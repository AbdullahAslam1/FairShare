# FairShare
Simplify shared expenses and personal budgets with FairShare: Flutter, Riverpod, and Supabase-based expense tracking app.

🚀 **Overview**  
FairShare is a mobile app built with **Flutter**, **Riverpod**, and **Supabase** that simplifies managing shared expenses and personal budgets. It’s designed to make expense tracking **transparent, collaborative, and intuitive** for groups and individuals alike.

---

🔹 Problem Solved
Managing shared expenses among friends, roommates, or travel groups can be messy and error-prone. FairShare ensures:  
- Clear visibility of who owes what  
- Simplified group management  
- Easy budgeting and tracking for personal and group finances  

---

## 🔹 Key Features
- **Secure Authentication:** Email & Google sign-in, password reset & change  
- **Group Management:** Create groups, add members, assign multiple admins  
- **Flexible Expense Splitting:** Equal, Percentage, or Custom amounts  
- **Expense Tracking:** Real-time settlement tracking, transparent group financial overview  
- **Budget Intelligence:** Individual & group budgets with monthly and weekly tracking  
- **Smart Home Dashboard:** Color-coded cards for debts (red) and receivables (green), visual spending charts
